Why it's built this way

  1. Loads after the cookie banner says yes. On a page with OneTrust, nothing renders until the chosen category is granted. Consent given after recording starts does not count.
  2. Age before name. A neutral year-of-birth question comes first. Under 13 stops. 13–17 goes to a parent path: the parent's name and email become the lead, flagged on_behalf_of_minor, email-only until the parent gives their own consent. The learner's identity and birth year are never stored, and ad click IDs are dropped on that path.
  3. Three unchecked boxes, named sender, not a condition of purchase. Email, text and call consent are separate, so reps only call people who said "call me."
  4. The receipt rides with the lead. Wording version, timestamp, page, checkbox state, GPC signal. Stored on the Salesforce record, not only in a vendor log.
  5. Straight to Salesforce. Web-to-Lead post, no middleman. Set the org ID and field IDs in the config block and it goes live.
